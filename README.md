# Goal Setter App

A hierarchical goal-setting application that helps you break down yearly goals into monthly, weekly, and daily actionable tasks.

## Overview

Goal Setter App helps you turn big dreams into achievable daily actions. Whether you want to run a marathon, learn a new skill, or build a business, this app provides a structured approach to make it happen.

## Key Features

### Hierarchical Goal Management
Break down your ambitious yearly goals into manageable pieces:
- **Yearly Goals**: Set your big-picture objectives for the year
- **Monthly Goals**: Define milestones to reach each month
- **Weekly Goals**: Plan specific tasks for each week
- **Daily Goals**: Focus on what you need to accomplish today

### Smart Progress Tracking
Track your progress in the way that makes sense for each goal:
- **Checkbox Tracking**: Simple done/not done for binary tasks
- **Numeric Tracking**: Track measurable progress (e.g., "Run 3 miles" with current progress 2/3 miles)
- **Hybrid Mode**: Combine both approaches for complex goals

### AI-Powered Guidance
Get intelligent suggestions to help you plan your goals:
- **Free Tier**: Guided prompts and templates to help you break down goals effectively
- **Paid Tier**: Full AI-powered goal planning that analyzes your timeline and provides personalized recommendations

### Progress Visualization
Stay motivated with clear visual feedback:
- Color-coded progress bars showing completion status
- Daily progress rolls up to weekly, monthly, and yearly achievements
- Weekly summary notifications showing how your daily work contributes to larger goals

### Focused Dashboard
- Daily goals displayed front and center
- Side panel for quick access to weekly, monthly, and yearly goals
- Clean, minimalist design that helps you focus on what matters

### Smart Notifications
- Weekly summary popups showing your progress
- See how your daily accomplishments contribute to monthly and yearly goals
- Celebrate wins and stay accountable

## How It Works

1. **Set Your Yearly Goals**: Define what you want to achieve this year
2. **Break Them Down**: Create monthly milestones, then weekly tasks, then daily actions
3. **Track Progress**: Mark tasks complete or update numeric progress each day
4. **Review Weekly**: Get automatic summaries showing how you're progressing toward larger goals
5. **Adjust and Achieve**: Refine your approach based on what's working

## Design Philosophy

Goal Setter App is built around the principle that big goals become achievable when broken into small, consistent actions. By focusing on daily progress while maintaining visibility of the bigger picture, you can build momentum and achieve what matters most to you.
